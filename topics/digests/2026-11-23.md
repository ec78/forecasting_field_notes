## What's new
- [Zero-shot TSFMs against the Khepri v1.5 baselines for 96-hour carbon-intensity forecasts in nine Nordic bidding zones: a preregistered comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02895) (Nicolaysen, arXiv 2 Nov 2026) — Timestral-5M/209M and Chronos-2 vs SARIMA and GBM baselines plus seasonal-naive; no covariates, 8192-hour context, design and minimum detectable effect timestamped before any foundation-model forecast. Snippet: each model beats SARIMA in 9 of 9 zones; GBM comparison and effect sizes not seen. Caveat flagged in the snippet: SARIMA quantiles are Gaussian from the fitted model, which may inflate the gap on probabilistic metrics. Grid carbon intensity is close kin to the CISO series. (Abstract snippet only.)

Search snippet also credits it with a finding that a classical baseline wins in 9 of 10 frequency/history combinations on M4 — unconfirmed. (Abstract not read.)
