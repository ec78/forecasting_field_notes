## What's new
- [ECHO: Correcting TSFM Forecasts with Historical Analogues and Errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16433) (Zhang et al., 12 Oct 2026) — black-box correction of a TSFM using retrieved historical analogues plus past forecast errors. Closest thing yet to Demo 2 (retrieval + residuals) applied to a frozen foundation model. Title + one-line summary only.
- [Hybrid neural-classical correction of a frozen TimesFM, high-frequency stocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08825) (Aug 2026; via RePEc listing) — reportedly finds a Random Forest residual learner is the largest single ablation contribution. Date/abstract unverified; relevant to the SPY series.
- [Reviving Error Correction in Modern Deep Time-Series Forecasting](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2605.21088) (May 2026) — revisits error-correction modules for deep forecasters; background for the residual-correction thread.
- [Time-Series Foundation Models That Understand Data Revisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28576) (Sep 2026, title only) — real-time/vintage data issues. No revised-vintage data in this repo, so tracked only.
- Pattern across ORCA (last week), ECHO, and the TimesFM+RF paper: black-box/residual correction of frozen TSFMs is a busy cluster — three of this repo's existing backlog threads (Demo 2, CTRL, ORCA) now have a direct peer.

## Candidate topics this week
- **Which correction signal helps a frozen forecaster: past errors, retrieved analogues, or both?** — source: [ECHO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16433), [ORCA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14222). Why: a 2x2 ablation (errors-only / analogues-only / both / neither) on Chronos-2 over CISO and SPY, reusing Demo 2's causal retrieval and the harness; the ablation is the honest question, not any paper's architecture. (Appended to backlog.)
- **Does a classical residual learner on a frozen TSFM help on SPY, or just fit noise?** — source: [TimesFM + RF paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08825). Why: SPY is near-random-walk; a small RF/ridge on Chronos-2 residuals, validation-tuned, tested on directional accuracy vs naive, is a cheap null-or-not test. (Appended to backlog.)
