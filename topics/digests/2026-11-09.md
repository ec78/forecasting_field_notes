## What's new
- [Post-Training in Time Series Foundation Models: A Unifying Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20002) (Jul 2026) — survey organising TSFM post-training by where it intervenes in the pipeline: parameter adaptation, context augmentation, model composition, output processing / uncertainty control, compression. Useful map of where Posts 01–04 sit (composition, residual/output correction) and what the series hasn't touched (uncertainty control).
- [It's TIME: Towards the Next Generation of Time Series Forecasting Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12147) (Qiao et al., ICML 2026) — 50 newly collected datasets / 98 tasks for leakage-free zero-shot TSFM evaluation, 12 models, pattern-level analysis, [HF leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Real-TSF/TIME-leaderboard). Context for the contamination question already parked in the backlog; not a demo candidate itself.
